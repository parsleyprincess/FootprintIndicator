## Demonstration examined

The official Bookmap WordPress media catalogue exposed a [Large Lot Tracker DAX demonstration](https://bookmap.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/02/Large-Lot-Tracker-Example-01-DAX-v021.mp4), attachment [2603](https://bookmap.com/wp-json/wp/v2/media/2603). The video was downloaded successfully from bookmap.com: 10,141,785 bytes, 1280×720, 30 frames/second, duration 138.925 seconds. It is a **2015 legacy demonstration**, not evidence of the current menu layout or present exchange capabilities.

The review decoded the video and inspected a chronological nine-frame contact sheet sampled at a 15-second cadence, plus a full-resolution frame extracted at 75 seconds. This is a frame-sequence inspection; the audio was not transcribed, and it should not be described as a review of every frame of a current-version tutorial.

Observed behavior:

- The window identifies an Eurex DAX contract. This directly demonstrates that the heatmap/time-price concept is not limited to the initially requested US index futures.
- Historical liquidity stays anchored to absolute prices as the latest bid/ask path moves and the chart scrolls. The main area advances while narrow current-book columns remain attached to the right edge.
- The grayscale palette shows long bright stationary bands and short changing blocks. A falling market does not move old horizontal bands down with its ladder position.
- Thin red/green bid/ask stair steps remain distinct from the small executed-volume circles. Volume changes marker size; a chart zoom/scroll should preserve underlying executions.
- The side area separates current quantity numbers, current order-book bars and a session-volume profile. They share a price scale but represent different statistics.
- Thin white marks inside certain current-book bars illustrate the legacy Large Lot Tracker presentation. The current LLT documentation explicitly describes an approximation; this video does not prove that those marks represented exchange order IDs or that the private estimator can be reconstructed.
- Later samples show a declining price path, then a rebound at a lower range. The right-hand live quantities change while previously recorded heat persists.

Local research-only assets: `/workspace/research-assets/motion/dax-large-lot-demo.mp4`, `dax-sequence.png`, and `dax-75s.png`. Original vendor media are not redistributed in the source package.
